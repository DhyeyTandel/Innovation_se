Horizons

H1 · Core

Extends and defends what already earns. Incremental, funds itself.

0–12 months · ~70% of portfolio

H2 · Adjacent

A new segment, channel, or redesign next to the core.

1–3 years · ~20% of portfolio

H3 · Transformational

An option on a future that doesn't exist yet. Small bet, high uncertainty.

3+ years · ~10% of portfolio

McKinsey's Three Horizons of Growth (Baghai, Coley & White, The Alchemy of Growth, 1999).

Readiness scale (1–9)

Adapted from NASA/DoD Technology Readiness Levels — hardware-only in the original, generalized here for processes and services.

Funneling techniques

Stage-gate

The five Board columns are checkpoints. Moving an idea across one is a decision, not a status update.

Weighted scoring

Desirability, feasibility, viability (IDEO), plus strategic fit — averaged into one 0–100 score per idea.

Horizon × TRL matrix

Shows whether the pipeline is stacked with safe, low-TRL H1 bets or missing H3 options entirely.

Threshold gate

One cutoff score on the Shortlist tab. Raise it to force-rank harder, lower it to keep more ideas alive.

Divergent techniques

SCAMPER, TRIZ, Jobs-to-be-Done, first-principles, analogical transfer — inside Ideation assist on any idea.
